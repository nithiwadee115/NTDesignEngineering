About Us

The Engineering Partner Leading Plants Rely On

N T Design Engineering Co., Ltd. was founded in March 2009 by Mr. Niyom Thongdee, Managing Director. Our business is Machining Parts & Made-to-Order engineering — from design and in-house manufacturing to on-site installation.

With our own CNC lathes and milling machines, an experienced technical team and 3D CAD design, we control quality, cost and lead time ourselves, without relying on subcontractors.

  • Quality control at every step

What services does NT Design Engineering provide?

We design and build special-purpose machines and automation systems, and provide CNC turning and milling, jigs and fixtures, control panels and electrical work, conveyor systems, piping systems, machine maintenance, as well as steel structures and civil works inside industrial plants.
